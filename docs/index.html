Why HTTP-200 Link Checkers Fail on Scientific Manuscripts

A valid DOI that returns HTTP 200 OK can still point to the wrong paper. On its first live evaluation of a 55-reference arXiv manuscript, rxiv-gate caught a subtle Crossref DOI conflation that passed standard bibliography linters.

Forensic Catch: Real Elsevier DOI, Wrong Paper

In references.bib, key degrandi2015fungicides cited DeGrandi-Hoffman et al. (2015) on honey bee hemolymph ATP depletion, but carried DOI 10.1016/j.jinsphys.2015.06.004. Because that DOI exists in Crossref, simple link checkers reported green.

Gate G3_CITATIONS queried the Crossref REST API, computed token similarity between the manuscript’s title and the registered metadata, and halted with CITATION_TITLE_MISMATCH (similarity = 0.00):

BibTeX Entry Title “Fungicides Reduce Hemolymph Protein and ATP Levels in Honey Bees (Apis mellifera)”
Disputed DOI in .bib 10.1016/j.jinsphys.2015.06.004 (HTTP 200 OK)
Actual Crossref Record “Functional analysis of Bombyx Wnt1 during embryogenesis using the CRISPR/Cas9 system” (Zhang et al., 2015)
Verified Replacement 10.1093/jee/tov251 (J. Econ. Entomol., similarity = 1.00)

Server-Specific Screening Matrices

Select your target preprint repository to inspect the exact moderator and automated compiler rules enforced by --server.

arXiv AutoTeX & Metadata Screening

  • Enforces arXiv’s strict 1,920-character Step-3 web abstract limit and flags raw \cite{} or \ref{} macros inside \begin{abstract}.
  • Detects parent-directory figure paths (../figures/), missing local figure files, machine-local paths (/Users/...), and forbidden \write18 shell-escape macros.
  • Verifies human-only authorship and transparent AI workflow disclosure in Methods or Acknowledgments per arXiv policy.

bioRxiv CSHL Life Sciences Screening

  • Requires original primary research data/simulations (rejects uninvited narrative reviews and purely speculative hypotheses).
  • Enforces mandatory Competing Interests, Data & Code Availability, and IRB / IACUC Ethics statements whenever human or animal samples are referenced.
  • Verifies that AlphaFold, AlphaGenome, gnomAD, GTEx, ChEMBL, and UniProt predictions are framed as computational candidates rather than wet-lab confirmed findings.

CLI Command for bioRxiv

python3 skills/preprint-readiness-gate/scripts/gate_runner.py \
  --manuscript-file paper.tex \
  --server biorxiv \
  --mode audit \
  --citation-mode online

medRxiv Enhanced Clinical & Patient Safety Gates

  • Prospective Clinical Trial ID Gate: Any interventional or randomized trial manuscript must include a valid NCTxxxxxxxx, ISRCTN, EudraCT, or WHO ICTRP registration identifier.
  • Ethics, Informed Consent & De-Identification: Enforces explicit IRB approval/exemption and patient de-identification declarations.
  • Clinical Directive Blocker: Blocks prescriptive clinical dosing or treatment directives aimed at patients in unpeer-reviewed preprints.

CLI Command for medRxiv

python3 skills/preprint-readiness-gate/scripts/gate_runner.py \
  --manuscript-file clinical_study.tex \
  --server medrxiv \
  --mode audit \
  --citation-mode online

ChemRxiv Chemical Structure & CADD Screening

  • Verifies citations for RDKit, AutoDock Vina / smina, OpenMM, PubChem, ChEMBL, and Boltz / AlphaFold 3 co-folding pipelines.
  • Enforces Competing Interests and machine-readable Data/Code Availability (SMILES/SDF/CIF repositories or Zenodo DOIs).
